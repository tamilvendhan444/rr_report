1. The Problem Statement & Our Mindset

When approaching Round 1 of the SupeRR Selector challenge, our mission was clear: can data genuinely isolate who rises when the game is on the line? Traditional player rankings rely heavily on aggregate metrics like career strike rate and raw batting average. However, in modern T20 cricket, these stats often create an illusion—rewarding players who pile on runs in low-leverage "garbage time" while penalizing death bowlers who take on the hardest micro-windows.

2. Our Journey Statement (Submission Narrative)

"Participating in SupeRR Selector Season 2 with Rajasthan Royals has been an exhilarating analytical journey!

Our biggest challenge was defining what genuine 'pressure' means in modern T20 cricket. Traditional stats like raw strike rate or economy often mislead due to low-leverage stat inflation in lost chases or dead rubbers. To solve this, we parsed 191,903 deliveries across 803 IPL and T20I matches, engineering a dynamic win-probability leverage index (LI ≥ 1.5x) and opposition-adjusted ridge regression.

The most fascinating breakthrough was discovering that true clutch performers—like Shreyas Iyer, Shubman Gill, and Josh Hazlewood (each with 93% bootstrap stability)—aren't unpredictable gamblers. Rather, their elite baseline simply does not dip when the game is on the line.

From statistical modeling to building an interactive public dashboard, this challenge pushed us to think like a real franchise war-room!"

3. The Breakthrough Moments

  • Moving to Win Probability Added (WPA): Normalizing ball values against match leverage allowed us to measure the exact shift in win equity on every single delivery.
  • The "Rise vs. Usual" Discovery: We realized that clutch performance isn't just about explosive spikes—it is defined by elite consistency. Players like Gill, Iyer, and Hazlewood prove that the highest form of reliability is refusing to drop below your standard under extreme stress.
  • Full-Stack Analytics Delivery: Rather than producing static spreadsheets, we built an end-to-end reproducible workflow: an econometric methodology report, automated bootstrap validations, and an interactive GitHub Pages dashboard.
